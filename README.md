**Repository name:** `Vcreation`

**GitHub description:**
> An affordable AI filmmaking studio for turning stories into cinematic videos, with reusable characters, storyboarding, dialogue, and timeline editing.

**README introduction:**
> Vcreation is an AI-powered creative studio designed to help filmmakers and storytellers produce short films and episodic dramas. It brings scriptwriting, character references, scene planning, video generation, voice production, and editing into one workspace—with a focus on character continuity, creative control, and transparent production costs.
